# Virtual Lab Platform v1.6

## Delivered
- Seven new L1 numerical demonstrations: logistic growth, first-order A→B kinetics, first-order environmental decay, one-compartment concentration decay (teaching only), and Lotka–Volterra predator–prey dynamics via RK4.
- Generalized parameter sweep, deterministic Monte Carlo parameter uncertainty, and comparison of selected solver output columns against user-supplied reference CSV data.
- Expanded scientific catalog, metadata-only official source pointers, and `/api/research/roadmap` describing the ten stages honestly.
- UI inputs for the new models and general analysis.

## Important limits
- L1 is numerical/software verification only, not empirical validation.
- No external model/dataset was downloaded or independently validated in this release. Source entries are pointers, not integrated data.
- No full cancer or Alzheimer model, no cosmetic safety model, no full SBML conformance, no physical hardware control and no AI planner.
- PK equation is educational only; it is not for dose selection or patient care.
